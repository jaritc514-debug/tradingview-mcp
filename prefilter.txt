PREFILTER SCAN — HTF Supply & Demand
=====================================
You are scanning the pair currently open on TradingView for fresh HTF Supply & Demand zones.
Quick scan only — no manual fib math, no entry calculation. See rules.json for the full rulebook.

---

STEP 1 — PULL ZONES (Weekly + Daily)
- chart_set_timeframe → Weekly
- data_get_pine_boxes(study_filter="Supply & Demand Pro", verbose=true) → record all active Weekly zones
- chart_set_timeframe → Daily
- data_get_pine_boxes(study_filter="Supply & Demand Pro", verbose=true) → record all active Daily zones
- For each zone, read directly from its tags (do not recompute): formation (RBD/RBR/DBR/DBD), F (flip), O (original), LoL (level on level)
- Freshness from bgColor: Fresh / 1st Touch / inactive (ignore inactive entirely) — see rules.json indicator_tags for exact bgColor values
- If the indicator returns no data: flag this pair as UNREADABLE and stop here

STEP 2 — HTF COVERAGE
For each Daily zone, check whether it sits inside or overlaps a Weekly zone of the same type (both supply or both demand).
HTF Coverage = Yes / No. Not mandatory — report both either way.

STEP 3 — LEG-OUT QUALITY
The indicator's own base-length filter is already capped at 6 candles — do not re-validate base length.
For each zone, pull the candles around its formation via data_get_ohlcv and classify the candle that broke the base:
  ≤49% body = indecisive | 50-75% = decisive | >75% (and abnormally large) = explosive
Leg-out quality = Explosive / Decisive. If the breaking candle reads as indecisive, flag as unusual — the indicator likely wouldn't have drawn this zone.

STEP 4 — TREND CONFLUENCE (tag only)
- data_get_pine_tables(study_filter="Trend Direction Pro") → read Daily and Weekly rows
- Record Trend (Bullish/Bearish), Status (Confirmed/Unconfirmed), Strength (Trending/Ranging) for both
- Tag each zone as trend-aligned or trend-conflicting. NEVER exclude a zone based on this.

STEP 5 — VALUATION CONFLUENCE (tag only, unverified)
- data_get_pine_tables(study_filter="Campus Valuation Tool") — if this fails, try data_get_study_values(study_filter="Campus Valuation Tool")
- This indicator's output format hasn't been confirmed live yet. Report whatever it returns as-is, and note in your output if the read attempt failed or looked malformed — that's useful signal for fixing rules.json.
- Tag only. NEVER exclude a zone based on this, even once verified.

STEP 6 — PROFIT CHECK
From each zone's edge, look for the nearest opposing zone (Fresh or 1st Touch) in the direction price would move out of the zone.
Path = Clear / Boxed in — [nearest opposing zone type] at [price range]

STEP 7 — RISK TIER
Apply the tier mapping from rules.json based on timeframe, freshness, leg-out quality, and HTF Coverage.
